The story

Most money apps ask for your bank login before they show you anything. Suba asks for nothing. You add a payment in a few taps, or import all your App Store subscriptions from a single screenshot that's read on your iPhone and never uploaded.

From there, Suba keeps watch. It reminds you before each renewal or bill, and before a free trial turns into a charge. It shows what your month really costs as a printed receipt, spots bills that quietly creep up, and lets you mark a payment paid in one tap from the Dynamic Island.

Suba itself is the guide: a small eye that follows your finger, blinks when you tap, sits in the Dynamic Island as you scroll, and celebrates when your month is fully paid. By day it lives under a bright sky; at night the moon comes out and stars drift by — sometimes one of them is one of your own subscriptions.

Designed for iPhone Duo

  • Wakes with the hinge. Suba's eye opens as the device unfolds, then stretches across the inner display.
  • Two panes. Payments on one side, details and the calendar on the other.
  • Around the camera. Closed, Suba sits around the outer camera; controls move to the new vertical bar.
  • Every pose. Partially folded, buttons move clear of the fold; set on a table, the month sits on top with "paid" buttons below.
  • StandBy. An extra-large widget shows this month's calendar under a day or night sky.

Features

  • Import subscriptions from a screenshot of the App Store, on device
  • Reminders before renewals, bills and free trials, at the time you choose
  • A monthly receipt of everything you pay, in your own currency (any currency, converted with daily rates)
  • Price history that flags bills creeping up
  • Calendar of every upcoming payment
  • 9 Home Screen widgets, a due-day Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts
  • iCloud sync across iPhone and iPad
  • Dark mode with its own night sky

Accessibility

Full VoiceOver support with custom actions, Dynamic Type up to the largest accessibility sizes, Reduce Motion that stills every animation, and payment status that never relies on color alone.

Localized from day one

Suba launches in 11 languages. Not just the words: each country gets its own currency, local services and sample data, and a fully localized App Store page.

About the developer

Bekir Ersever is an independent iOS developer who designs, builds and markets his apps alone. Suba is his answer to a simple frustration: a subscription tracker shouldn't itself be a subscription, and shouldn't need your bank password.
